## About

I am a PhD Candidate in Computer Science at **Queensland University of Technology (QUT)**, affiliated with the **ARC Training Centre for Automated Vehicles in Rural and Remote Regions (AVR3)**. My research sits at the intersection of human-centred AI, explainability, natural language processing, and automated driving.

My thesis develops a framework for generating human-centred explanations for automated vehicles — turning complex autonomous decisions into natural language that people can understand, evaluate, and trust.

## Research Focus

```text
Focus    ›  Human-Centred Explainable AI (XAI)
Domain   ›  Automated Vehicles · NLP · LLMs · Computer Vision
Goal     ›  Trustworthy, understandable autonomous driving decisions
```

| | Area | Description |
|--|------|-------------|
| 🧠 | **Explainable AI** | Turning AV decisions into structured, justifiable explanations |
| 💬 | **NLP & LLMs** | Linguistic building blocks and language generation for AV decisions |
| 🚗 | **Automated Vehicles** | Perception, scene context, and sensor evidence pipelines |
| 👥 | **Human-Centred AI** | Trust calibration, human evaluation, and explanation design |
| 🔬 | **Psycholinguistics** | Grounding XAI design in how people process and interpret language |
